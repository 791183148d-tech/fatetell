Da Yun: Your 10-Year Life Cycles (大运)

Da Yun (大运), meaning "Great Fortune" or "Big Transport", represents the 10-year cycles that shape your life's journey. Each cycle brings a different elemental energy that interacts with your birth chart, creating favorable or challenging periods for different areas of life.

How Da Yun Works

Your first Da Yun cycle starts at a specific age (calculated from your birth data and gender). Each subsequent cycle lasts 10 years. The pillar (Heavenly Stem + Earthly Branch) of each cycle interacts with your natal chart — a Wood cycle may bring growth to a Wood Day Master, or challenge to an Earth Day Master (Wood controls Earth).
