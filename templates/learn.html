What is BaZi?

BaZi (八字) literally means "Eight Characters" — the eight Chinese characters that represent your birth moment: year, month, day, and hour. Each of these four pillars consists of a Heavenly Stem (天干) and an Earthly Branch (地支), creating a unique energetic blueprint.

Think of BaZi as a cosmic user manual. It doesn't predict a fixed destiny, but reveals your natural tendencies, strengths, challenges, and the best timing for different life pursuits. It answers questions like:

  • What are my natural talents and how should I develop them?
  • Why do I click with some people and clash with others?
  • When is the right time for a career change, marriage, or major investment?
  • What life lessons will each decade bring?
