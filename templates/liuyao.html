📖 About Liu Yao (六爻)

Liu Yao — "Six Lines" — is a method of I Ching divination using three coins tossed six times. Each toss produces a Yin (broken) or Yang (solid) line. The six lines build a hexagram from the bottom up, creating one of 64 possible archetypal situations. Lines that land as "old yin" (three tails) or "old yang" (three heads) are changing lines — they transform into their opposite, producing a second hexagram that shows the direction of change.
