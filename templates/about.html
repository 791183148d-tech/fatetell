Our Mission

FateTell brings the ancient wisdom of Chinese BaZi (Four Pillars of Destiny) to the modern world through the power of AI. We believe that understanding your energetic blueprint is not about fatalism — it's about self-awareness, timing, and personal growth.

Our AI combines 2,000+ years of BaZi tradition with modern psychological insights to deliver readings that are both spiritually grounded and practically useful.

How Our AI Works

  1. BaZi Calculation — Your birth date and time are converted into the Four Pillars using traditional lunar calendar algorithms.
  2. Five Elements Analysis — The system calculates the balance of Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water in your chart.
  3. Ten Gods Mapping — Each of the eight characters is mapped to its corresponding spiritual influence (Shi Shen).
  4. Da Yun Calculation — Your 10-year life cycles are computed based on your gender and birth data.
  5. AI Interpretation — A large language model (Claude/DeepSeek) generates your personalized reading based on all this data.
